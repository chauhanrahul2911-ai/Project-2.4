Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. What Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and profile photo from Google, via our authentication provider (Supabase). We do not receive or store your Google password.

Your quiz progress, scores, and completion status are stored locally in your browser (localStorage) on your own device.

If you purchase Premium access, your Google account ID and premium status are stored in our database (Supabase), so your unlock is remembered if you sign in again on a different device.

We do not collect or store your card, UPI, or bank details — payments are handled entirely by Razorpay, and card/banking data never passes through our own systems.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To let you sign in and identify your account
  • To remember your Premium purchase across devices
  • To provide customer support if you contact us

We do not sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

  • Google Sign-In — for authentication
  • Supabase — for storing account and premium-status data securely
  • Razorpay — for processing payments

Each of these services has its own privacy policy governing how they handle data.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your account data is stored with access controls that only allow you to read your own information — no other user can access your data. Reasonable technical measures are used to protect stored data, though no online system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You can stop using the platform and sign out at any time. If you would like your account data permanently deleted from our database, contact us at the email below and we will process the request.

6. Cookies & Local Storage

This platform uses your browser's local storage (not third-party tracking cookies) to remember your login state and quiz progress. Clearing your browser data will remove this locally stored information from that device.

7. Children's Privacy

This platform is intended for exam aspirants generally aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the platform after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

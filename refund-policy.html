Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Nature of Our Product

Premium test series on this platform are digital content that is unlocked instantly upon successful payment. Because access is granted immediately and the content can be viewed right away, we are generally unable to offer refunds once Premium access has been unlocked.

2. When a Refund IS Available

If your payment was successfully deducted from your bank/UPI/card, but Premium access was not unlocked on your account due to a technical error on our end, you are entitled to either:

  • A manual unlock of your Premium access once we verify the payment, or
  • A full refund of the amount paid, if you'd prefer not to wait for a manual unlock.

To raise this, contact us within 7 days of the payment, with your payment ID/transaction reference and the Google account email you used. Approved refunds will be processed back to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.

3. When a Refund is NOT Available

  • Change of mind after successfully accessing Premium content
  • Not clearing or not attempting the actual exam
  • Dissatisfaction with exam results
  • Requests raised after 7 days from the date of payment

4. How to Request

Email us with your payment ID (visible in your Razorpay payment confirmation) and a brief description of the issue. We aim to respond within 2–3 business days.
